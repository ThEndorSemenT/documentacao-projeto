6. TAO

A Tokenized Asset Organization (TAO) aligns financial incentives with meaning by leveraging real-world assets (RWAs), or digital tokens, that represent goods or services.

When coupled with the principles of a Purpose Economy, TAOs enhance liquidity, broaden access to capital and foster a sense of community. This approach ensures that each contribution to society's well being is valued regardless of authority.
